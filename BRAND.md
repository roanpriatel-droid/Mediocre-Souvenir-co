# MEDIOCRE SOUVENIR CO. — Brand System

**Doc. No. 1978-B · Rev. 2** — distilled from the brand identity system.
This file is law. Every design decision passes one test:
**would a sincere 1978 gift shop in this town have done it this way?**

## Positioning

Faux-vintage souvenir tees for unglamorous, overlooked towns across Canada
and the US. The joke is that these towns don't deserve souvenir merch and we
make it anyway — treating a town of 12,000 with the exact reverence a Hawaii
gift shop would. Deadpan, dry, affectionate. **Never mean toward the places.
The humor is entirely in the sincerity.**

Tagline: **Genuine Merch for Overlooked Places** · Est. 2026 · North America

## Type

- **Display:** Alfa Slab One — signage, town names, headings. Weight 400 only.
- **Body:** Archivo Narrow — postcard-back energy. ≥15px on mobile, 17px base.
- **Accent:** Permanent Marker — small handwritten hits only ("est. 2026,"
  stamps, margin notes). Never for paragraphs. Usually rotated −2° to −3°.
- Display sizes get letter-spacing tightened; labels/kickers get
  2–4px letter-spacing and uppercase.

## Design language

- Sun-bleached, faded, slightly worn, lovingly kept. Everything looks 30
  years old on purpose. **If it looks designed in 2026, it's wrong.**
- NO gradients, no soft/glossy drop shadows, no neons, no modern polish.
- Hard offset shadows only (`3px 3px 0 navy`), 2–3px asphalt borders,
  dashed rules, dotted leaders, punch holes, stamps at a slight rotate.
- Worn fractal-noise overlay at low opacity (multiply) across the site.
- Off-register print effects (dual-color text-shadow) for hero display type.
- Motion: subtle and mechanical — opacity/translate only, ≤400ms, always
  behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Product

- Blank: **Comfort Colors 1717** garment-dyed heavyweight — the blank does
  the faded-vintage work. Never substitute a standard Bella+Canvas.
- Unisex S–3XL. Colorways: Ivory, Butter, Blue Jean, Brick, Sage.
- Price: **$36 CAD / $36 USD** (parity). Ladder: 2+ save 15%, 3+ save 20%,
  automatic, mix-and-match, no code.
- Three shirt templates: **A Classic Varsity** (arched town, landmark, est.
  year) · **B Retro Postcard** ("greetings from…", souvenir stripe) ·
  **C Faded Slogan** (one deadpan line at 88% ink).

## Voice

We say: "A commemorative garment for a town of 11,942." · "Wear it with the
quiet pride it deserves." · "Sarnia has a bridge. It's a good bridge."

We never say: "lol this town sucks" · "The ironic gift for your weird
friend!" · anything with an exclamation point · anything that explains the joke.

Fixed strings: cart = "Your souvenirs" · empty cart = "Nothing collected
yet." · order confirmation = "Thanks for visiting." · 404 = "There's nothing
here. Fitting." · email = "Postcards from us."

## Trust rules

Never invent reviews, counts, ratings, or press. Empty states stay honest
("The guest book is open."). Trust is carried by: the garment spec, the
return policy, the ladder, and the sincerity of the whole thing.
